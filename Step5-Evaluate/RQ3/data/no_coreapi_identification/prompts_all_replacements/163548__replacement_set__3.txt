Issue ID: 163548
Title: DefaultSavePlanner._validate_global_planis costly slow when FSDP size is extremely large (quadratic)
Bug Description:
### 🚀 The feature, motivation and pitch

## Problem
[`_validate_global_plan`](https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/blob/bf28990c3dd4d8bfa3eeb36a69cf53ed5f9e583e/torch/distributed/checkpoint/default_planner.py#L624) has O(n²) complexity that becomes unusably slow for modern LLMs. With 1024 tensors and `fsdp_size` 1024, validation takes 200+ seconds. if `fsdp_size` is 2048,  this can take 10+ minutes, making it unusable in production.

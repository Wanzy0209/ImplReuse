```python
    GraphTransformObserver(graph, "joint_custom_pre_pass").apply_graph_pass(
        config.joint_custom_pre_pass
    )
    count += 1
```

joint_custom_pre_pass is executed twice.

By looking at the blame, I find commit [bf44d5bfb558b61e](https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/commit/bf44d5bfb558b61e80601784a3d1a053ff574011) moved `joint_custom_pre ` , but commit [92b7e610abf3](https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/commit/92b7e610abf3e3ea3cded019a0976718675d480f) just add a new `joint_custom_pre` invoke without removing the old one.

So I think it may be a mistake when merging code into main branch.
